Exploratory Data Analysis - Housing Development Board

This project explores Singapore’s Housing Development Board (HDB) resale flat prices to uncover patterns and trends influencing property values across different towns, flat types, and years. The goal was to understand how factors such as flat size, location, and lease duration affect housing prices, providing valuable insights for potential buyers, policy analysts, and real-estate decision-making. Using Python and Jupyter Notebook, I performed data cleaning, preprocessing, and exploratory analysis with libraries such as Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Seaborn. The analysis included identifying missing values, feature transformations, and visualizing correlations between variables. I applied descriptive statistics and trend analysis to reveal key insights — such as price growth patterns over time, top-performing towns, and the relationship between floor area and resale price. This project strengthened my skills in data wrangling, exploratory data analysis, and data visualization, demonstrating how raw public data can be transformed into actionable insights. It also reflects my ability to communicate findings effectively through clean visuals and evidence-based narratives — a core aspect of analytical storytelling.

Cosmetics Sales Performance Dashboard

This project focuses on analyzing and visualizing sales performance for a cosmetics retail business to help management make data-driven decisions. The objective was to monitor key sales metrics, identify top-performing products, regions, and sales channels, and highlight areas requiring strategic improvement. Using Power BI and Excel, I designed an interactive dashboard that consolidated raw sales data into meaningful KPIs such as total revenue, profit margin, customer segmentation, and product category performance. The project involved data cleaning, transformation, and creating calculated measures using DAX functions. Visual elements like bar charts, line graphs, and dynamic slicers were incorporated to enable users to filter insights by time period, product type, and region. Through this project, I strengthened my ability to perform business intelligence reporting, data visualization, and performance tracking. It showcases my capability to transform complex datasets into intuitive dashboards that support quick and effective business decisions — bridging the gap between data analysis and strategic action.

Customer Churn Analysis(Telecom)

This project aimed to analyze customer churn behavior in a telecom company to identify key factors influencing customer retention and develop data-driven insights to reduce churn rates. The goal was to understand how customer demographics, service usage patterns, and contract types impact churn decisions. Using Python in Jupyter Notebook, I performed data cleaning, feature engineering, and exploratory analysis with libraries like Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Seaborn. Statistical summaries and correlation analysis were used to uncover patterns between variables such as tenure, monthly charges, and contract type. Visualizations helped highlight key drivers of churn, including high billing amounts and short-term contracts. This project enhanced my skills in data preprocessing, exploratory data analysis, and insight generation, while strengthening my ability to interpret customer behavior through data. It reflects my capability to translate analytical findings into actionable business recommendations — a crucial aspect of customer retention strategy in data-driven industries.
